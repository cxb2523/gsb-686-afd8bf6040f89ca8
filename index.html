Use it

  1. Python 3.9 - 3.12 and ffmpeg on your PATH.
  2. Start run.bat (or run.ps1, run.sh). The first run sets itself up: a virtual environment, PyTorch, TensorFlow and the models.
  3. Select a face, select a target (a picture or a video), press Start and pick where the result goes.

Where it comes from

Changeling Imago grew out of roop by s0md3v, and like roop it's free software under the GNU AGPL-3.0. Its NSFW check stays on. Use it on faces you have the right to use: AI-generated people, yourself, or people who agreed to it.
